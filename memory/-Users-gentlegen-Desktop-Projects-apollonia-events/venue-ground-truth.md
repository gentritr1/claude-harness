Apollonia Events is in **Gjilan, Kosovo** (Rr. Demush Shabani, 60000 Gjilan), NOT near Fier,
Albania. Phones are +383. "Apollonia" is only the *name's* origin — the ancient city near Fier is
a namesake, not the neighbourhood. The owner had every "buzë Adriatikut" reference removed.

Other owner-confirmed facts (source: `VENUE-QUESTIONS.md`, answers section):
- **One hall, ~60 m²**, which becomes three rooms across a day: ceremony → dinner → dancing.
- Capacity **30–35 seated, 50–60 standing**. No minimum. It is an *intimate* venue, not a banquet hall.
- Room is **grey**: grey tile floor, grey-white walls, black-and-white marble counter, and a
  **ceiling worked as an artwork** (the owner names it the distinctive feature). No outdoor space.
  Note the tension with the warm ivory/gold brand palette.
- **No kitchen** — client brings their own catering, drinks and decor. Venue supplies space + sound/DJ.
- **Up to 2 events per day.** The "one event at a time" promise was removed everywhere — do not
  build scarcity mechanics on it.
- One person handles bookings, **replies within 24h** (confirmed promise). No coordinator.
- 50% deposit; cancellation 48–72h ahead. Music full volume until 23:00 (residential), guests
  until 00:00–01:00. Visitor picks any half-hour slot 10:00–22:00.
- Instagram @apollonia.events; WhatsApp +383 44 376 237.
