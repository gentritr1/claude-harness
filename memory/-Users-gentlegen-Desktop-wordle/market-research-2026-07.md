**Competitors (all observed live 2026-07-16):** fjaleza.com (digraphs single-tile via auto-merge, word meanings, archive, share — closest rival), fjalth.com (dedicated digraph keys, cleanest execution, 4.5/5 UI), fjalez.al (5/6/7-letter tiers, but splits digraphs — wrong; no mobile keyboard), fjalez.metinferati.com (splits digraphs, archive), Luaj Live (ad farm, 1.5/5). No quality native app exists; field is web-first. THREE competitors named Fjalëz/Fjalëza + fjalth — "FJALË" name sits in a crowded namespace; the "36" descriptor matters.

**Positioning verdict:** "digraphs done right" is CONTESTED (fjalth + fjaleza both do it). Defensible bundle = editorial depth (definitions/examples/clues — nobody has it beyond fjaleza's bare meaning link) + dictionary trust + passport/Besa meta + explicit accessibility. "No ads" is table stakes among serious rivals, not a wedge.

**User-sentiment top signals (mostly transferable/general; Albanian community sentiment is thin — itself evidence of an unowned market):**
1. Dictionary trust is binary — one wrongly rejected real word kills trust permanently. Large guess list ≠ curated answers. Gheg variants (Kosovo/N.Macedonia/diaspora) are the Albanian-specific landmine: accept them as guesses, teach the standard form, never flat-reject.
2. Streak-loss anxiety = #1 churn driver; forgiveness mechanics are loved. (Roadmap bans "streak freezes" in the monetization bundle — a FREE grace mechanic is a separate question, needs user decision.)
3. Diaspora heritage-learners = richest unserved segment (no Albanian on Duolingo is a documented frustration); they play for identity/meaning, not difficulty.
4. Free no-account archive = real differentiator (NYT paywall backlash). Protect it.
5. Hard mode: ~30% of engaged players want it; doubles as vocab training.
6. Heavy animations = lowest-ROI direction; anti-gimmick sentiment is real. Restrained polish is correct.

**Recommended follow-ups:** hands-on teardown of fjaleza.com + fjalth.com (log dictionary/forgiveness/definition failures → gap spec); device paste-test of share grid in WhatsApp/Viber; read app-store reviews + r/albania threads directly when possible (Reddit was unfetchable for the swarm).
