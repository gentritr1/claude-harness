Round two (2026-09-30) overturned all three round-one picks:
- Flat-price Shopify app: a scan of 851 apps in 48 categories found a free/cheap flat app already on page one of every category.
- Daggerheart companion: DPCGL 2.0 permits selling only in print/streaming/podcast/approved-VTT formats; VTTs "may not be monetized"; the name cannot be in a title (licence PDF read directly).
- Developer API: no countable pain; free libraries cover it.
Also established: people in Kosovo cannot buy on Google Play (free downloads only); iOS leads mobile page views in Kosovo and Albania.

Round two result (published as artifact Version 2 on 2026-09-30, same URL; report `reports/Indie app ideas build plan.md`):
- Critic killed 21 of 32 (66%), devil's advocate 25 of 31 (81%); no idea reached High confidence; none has a paying customer.
- Recommended: Bet 1 = Google Sheets add-on that reliably pulls Jira issues (incumbent: Atlassian's free Jira Cloud for Sheets,
  3.35M installs, 3.35 stars, last updated 2024-12-16; 14 of 16 low-star reviews since 2024 say broken), sold via Paddle on the
  friend's Polish account; Bet 2 = Europe-only Android pollen app (widget, one-time unlock) on Open-Meteo's commercial plan
  (Google Pollen API forbids caching); N0 = measure the existing games and word game first, then an ad-format test and the
  word game as an app on whichever phones its visitors use (Kosovo is ~64-70% iOS by page views).
- Plan: ~70 person-days in 4 stages with stop rules T1-T16; Stage 0 is free checks (own metrics, account/billing answers,
  M1 and A1 kill checks).
